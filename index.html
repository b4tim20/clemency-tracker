Schema

Method

  • Spine: U.S. Department of Justice, Office of the Pardon Attorney — official grant lists for both terms.
  • Cross-check: Wikipedia's two clemency lists, matched by normalized name; agreement promotes a row to Verified.
  • January 6, 2025: Proclamation 10887 (Federal Register 2025-01950) — the blanket grant is one aggregate row; the 14 individuals commuted by name are listed individually.
  • Deduplication: by name + type + date, so a person commuted and later pardoned keeps both actions.
Limitations, stated rather than buried: 95 records are single-source and flagged Unverified; they are retained, not dropped. Political_Party is Unknown for most rows because DOJ does not publish it and it was not inferred. Restitution_Amount is populated only where the sentence text states a figure. The roster of roughly 1,500 individually pardoned January 6 defendants has not been published, so it is represented as an aggregate.
